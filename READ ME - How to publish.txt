2434 (Church Fenton) Squadron - Training Hub
============================================
Publishing to Netlify


WHAT TO DRAG
  Drag THIS ENTIRE FOLDER onto app.netlify.com/drop
  Not the files inside it, and not a zip. The whole folder.

  Netlify serves index.html automatically, which is the Training Hub.


THE STEPS
  1. Go to  app.netlify.com/drop
     No account needed to try it. You will want one to keep the URL.
  2. Drag this folder onto the dashed drop area.
  3. Wait about twenty seconds. You get a URL like
        https://spontaneous-otter-4a1b2c.netlify.app
  4. Rename it: Site configuration > Change site name > e.g. 2434sqn-nav
     giving you  https://2434sqn-nav.netlify.app

  That URL is now the squadron's training hub. Share it with cadets.


IMPORTANT - DO THIS BEFORE CADETS USE IT
  Straight after publishing, the hub runs on SAMPLE DATA. Anyone visiting can
  sign in as anybody, and nothing they do is saved anywhere real.

  To make it a working system:
  1. Create a free project at supabase.com - choose the London (eu-west-2)
     region, so cadet data stays in the UK.
  2. In Supabase, open the SQL Editor, paste in the whole of hub-schema.sql
     (it is in this folder) and press Run.
  3. Read the note at the very bottom of hub-schema.sql - it tells you how to
     make yourself the first staff account. This is the one step you have to do
     by hand, because only staff can create staff and there are no staff yet.
  4. Open the published hub, click SET UP THE DATABASE, and paste in the
     Project URL and the anon public key from Supabase
     (Project Settings > API).

  Each person does step 4 once on their own device - the keys are stored in
  that browser. If you would rather bake them in for everyone, tell me and I
  will put them in the code instead, which is safe: the anon key is designed to
  be public, and the security comes from the rules in hub-schema.sql.


UPDATING LATER
  Netlify > your site > Deploys > drag the folder on again. It replaces the
  previous version. Cadet accounts and their work live in Supabase, not in
  these files, so nothing is lost by redeploying.


WHAT IS IN HERE
  index.html          the Training Hub itself
  hub-data.js         the only file that talks to the database
  hub-schema.sql      paste this into Supabase (step 2 above)
  support.js          framework the hub is built on
  lessons/            the five lessons - slides pulled from the PowerPoints
  anim/               the 18 animations, plus their own library page
  media/              squadron crest

  Every file must stay in the folder together.


A NOTE ON CADET DATA
  The hub deliberately stores as little as possible: a service number and a
  display name. No dates of birth, no addresses. An email address is used once,
  to check a cadet is who they say they are when they sign up, and is erased
  automatically the moment they claim their account. Passwords are handled by
  Supabase and never touch these files.
